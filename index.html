How to Contribute to Kudla on OpenStreetMap

Add missing local shops, water points, repairs, or toilets in 3 simple steps

1

Open OpenStreetMap iD Editor

Launch the built-in web editor centered on Mangalore. Log in or create a free account with Google/GitHub/email.

2

Place a Point

Click Point at top, drop the marker on the building or shop, and search for the category (e.g. Hardware, Pharmacy, Toilet).

3

Add Details & Local Names

Add phone, opening hours, address, and local language tags: name:kn (Kannada) and name:tcy (Tulu).

Recommended OSM Tags for Everyday Kudla Utilities

Utility Category OpenStreetMap Tag Example Values & Notes
Hardware & Tools shop=hardware / paint / electrical Pipes, electrical wires, sanitaryware, building tools
Repair Shops shop=electronics_repair / bicycle_repair Mobile screens, laptops, bikes, cobbler (craft=shoemaker)
Stationery & Xerox shop=stationery / copyshop / books Photocopy, spiral binding, college supplies
Pharmacies amenity=pharmacy Add opening_hours=24/7 if open round-the-clock
Public Toilets amenity=toilets Add fee=yes/no and wheelchair=yes/no
Drinking Water amenity=drinking_water Free public water kiosks / RO water dispensers
Bus Stops highway=bus_stop City bus stop shelters & stage names
