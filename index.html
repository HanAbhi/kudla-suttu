Contribute to OpenStreetMap Kudla

Help build the most accurate, open community directory for Mangalore

Kudla Suttu pulls real-time civic data directly from OpenStreetMap (OSM) — the free, editable world map. Any detail you add or fix on OSM becomes permanently available to everyone across Mangalore and the world.

1

Open the iD Editor

Click below to launch OpenStreetMap's built-in web editor centered on Mangalore. You can sign up with your Google, GitHub, or OSM account in 1 minute.

2

Add a Point or Building

Click "Point" at the top of the editor, place it on your shop or facility, and search for the feature type.

3

Add Accurate Tags & Multilingual Names

Fill in the name, street address, contact phone, opening hours, and add Kannada/Tulu name fields: name:kn and name:tcy.

Common OSM Tags for Kudla Essentials

shop=hardware Hardware & building tools
shop=electrical Electrical items & wiring
shop=plumbing Plumbing pipes & sanitaryware
shop=paint Paints & painting supplies
craft=shoemaker / tailor Shoe repairs & stitching
amenity=toilets Public or pay-and-use toilets
amenity=drinking_water Public clean drinking water point
name:kn=* / name:tcy=* Kannada / Tulu place names
